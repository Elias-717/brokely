# Brokely — Student Discovery Interview Guide

Purpose: confirm the problem before 0.1 is locked (PRD v1.2 section 12) and gather evidence for the open research questions (PRD section 14).
Format: 5 students, 20–30 minutes each, one-to-one, in person or a call.
Interview notes contain personal data: keep them outside this public repo.

## Rules for the interviewer
- Ask about **what they actually did**, not what they would do ("Tell me about the last time…", not "Would you use…?").
- **Don't pitch Brokely** until section 8. Opinions about an idea are weak evidence; past behaviour is strong evidence.
- Ask "Why?" and "Can you show me?" (their banking or TNG app history, a notes app, a spreadsheet).
- Write down exact quotes. Don't record names in your notes; use "Student 1–5".
- Ask permission first: "This is for my project. I won't share your name or exact amounts."

## 1. Background (2 min)
1. What year and course are you in? Do you live in the hostel?
2. How do you get money for daily spending? (Monthly allowance, scholarship, part-time job, irregular transfers?) Which day does it usually arrive?

## 2. Last week's spending (5 min)
3. Walk me through everything you spent money on yesterday. How did you pay for each one?
4. What do you spend on in a normal week? Which costs come back every week or month?
5. Essentials, one at a time:
   - **Food:** how many meals do you buy a day, and what does a typical meal cost?
   - **Laundry:** how often do you wash, and what does one wash (and dry) cost?
   - **Transport:** Grab, bus, anything else? How often?
   - **Phone and data:** how much, and how often do you top up?
   - **Subscriptions:** anything paid monthly (Spotify, Netflix, software)?

## 3. Estimation exercise (3 min) — PRD open question 2
6. Without checking anything, how much do you think you spend on **food in a month**? On **laundry**?
7. Now let's check your TNG or bank history for last week. *(Compare it with their estimate and note the gap.)* Is that what you expected?

## 4. Running short (3 min)
8. Tell me about the last time money got tight before your next allowance. What happened, and what did you cut?
9. How do you know if you're spending too fast during the month?
10. How often do you check your balance, and in which app?

## 5. Tracking tools (3 min)
11. Have you ever tracked your spending (app, notes, spreadsheet)? Which one?
12. How long did you keep it up? Why did you stop, or what keeps you going?
13. What was the most annoying part of tracking?

## 6. Friends and debts (3 min)
14. When did you last pay for a friend, or a friend paid for you? How did you remember who owes what?
15. How often does this happen in a normal week?
16. Have you ever forgotten a debt, or lost money because of it?

## 7. Payments and notifications (3 min) — PRD open questions 3 and 4
17. Roughly what share of your spending is TNG, cash, bank card or bank transfer? For TNG: mostly QR payments or transfers to people?
18. Which app notifications do you find useful, and which annoy you? How many reminders a day from a money app would be OK?

## 8. Fun and the mascot (3 min) — PRD open question 5
19. Do you use any app with streaks, levels or a mascot (Duolingo, a fitness app, a game)? Does it actually keep you coming back?
20. *(Show the Brokely broccoli sketch and one example notification.)* First reaction? Would this make you log more, or would it feel childish?

## 9. Wrap-up (1 min)
21. If an app could do one thing to help with your money, what would it be?
22. Who else should I talk to?
