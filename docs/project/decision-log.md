# Brokely — Decision Log

Decisions D-01 to D-09 and R-01 to R-08 were taken on 19 Oct 2026 while reviewing PRD v1.0 and SRS v1.0, and are applied in v1.1. Decisions D-10 to D-21 and R-09 to R-10 are applied in SRS/PRD v1.2 (see SRS section 15, change record). Later decisions go into the next revision. Evidence references (F-xx) point to the private research log, which is not in this public repo.

## Calculation rules

| # | Rule | Chosen option | Implications |
|---|------|---------------|--------------|
| R-01 | Coverage | "No spending today" check-in. | A day is covered if it has a spending entry or a check-in. Coverage = covered days ÷ elapsed days. |
| R-02 | Forecast formula | Split formula. | Forecast = spent so far + scheduled items still to come + everyday rate × days left. The everyday rate leaves out transactions linked to scheduled items. |
| R-03 | Rounding | Round only when displaying. | Exact whole sen for every sum; division results rounded half-even only for display. |
| R-04 | Sign convention | Positive amount plus a type. | amount_sen > 0; the type decides the effect. Refunds reference the purchase they reverse. |
| R-05 | Paying a planned bill | "Mark paid" in 0.1, reminder in 0.2. | Mark paid creates a linked transaction with an editable amount; the item leaves "still to come". |
| R-06 | Elapsed days | Today counts only once it is covered. | Elapsed days = cycle days before today, plus today if covered. |
| R-07 | Debt write-off | A receivable write-off becomes spending. | Recorded in a category the user chooses, on the write-off date. A payable write-off only closes the debt. |
| R-08 | Pace | Derived from the forecast. | "Over pace" if forecast > planned spending; hidden when the forecast is hidden. |
| R-09 | Forecast gate and range | From **day 14** (coverage ≥ 70%), shown as a **range**. | Replaces the 7-day gate. Range method: see R-10. Pace uses the middle of the range. |
| R-10 | Forecast range method | Everyday-rate part ±15%, rounded to RM10. | Low = N + still to come + 0.85 × everyday rate × days left, rounded **down** to RM10; high = same with 1.15, rounded **up** to RM10. The unrounded middle (×1.00) is used for pace. 15% to be tuned with pilot data. |
| R-11 | Pace before categories have amounts (19 Oct) | **Compare with the budget until any spending category has an amount.** | Pace target = P if P > 0, otherwise B (BR-21). Needed by quick start (D-23), where P starts at 0 and pace would otherwise always say over pace. `paceTarget` in packages/domain; AC-56. |
| R-12 | Catch-up progress window (19 Oct) | **Count from the day tracking starts.** | Covered days, elapsed days, days left, daily average and everyday rate use only days from `tracking_start_date`; spending before it still counts in N, left in plan and the forecast's N (BR-29). Without this the forecast could never appear in a catch-up month. |
| R-13 | Wallet check formula (19 Oct) | **Expected = last check + money in − money out since.** | Money in: income, borrow, repayment received, refund. Money out: purchase (full amount, even when split with a friend), lend, repayment made. Neither: transfer, write-off (BR-31). Difference = typed − expected. |
| R-14 | Money not planned yet, after spending (20 Oct) | **Spent money counts as spoken for.** For an essential card, "not planned yet" = B − Σ over categories of max(Cᵢ, Nᵢ): whichever is bigger, the amount planned or the amount already spent in that category (set-asides included). A card's amount covers the plan's tracked days (R-12), so **spending dated before tracking starts** (the catch-up "Spent before Brokely" entry) is added on top of the card amount for that category. | Found by the author: catch up with RM400 and RM360 spent, then meals RM78 said "RM322 not planned" instead of "RM38 over". Worked examples: catch-up mostly Meals → Meals RM438, RM38 over; mostly Other → RM38 over; normal month with RM40 snacks spent → RM60 left after RM300 meals. Left in plan (B − N, BR-12) and the shortfall rule (FR-PLN-10, B − ΣC) are unchanged. |
