# Brokely Glossary (v1.5)

Plain meanings only. The exact formulas live in the SRS business rules (section 5).

## Your plan
- **Budget**: how much you plan to spend this cycle. Not your bank balance.
- **Cycle**: the dates your plan covers, e.g. 1–30 Nov.
- **Category**: a group of spending, like Meals or Laundry. It's either essential or optional.
- **Home necessities**: groceries, toiletries and cleaning things for your room or home (replaces "Groceries & toiletries", D-43).
- **Your own category**: a category you name yourself, by typing under Other; it's offered again next time (D-44).
- **Allocation**: how much of your budget you give a category.
- **Set-aside**: money in your plan you don't mean to spend, like "Emergency".
- **Unallocated**: budget you haven't given to anything yet.
- **Shortfall**: when you've planned more than your budget.
- **Revision**: a changed version of your plan. The first version is always kept.
- **Quick start**: the fastest way to make a plan: just your budget and dates. You can add essentials later.

## Your money
- **Transaction**: one thing you recorded, like "RM8 lunch".
- **Spending**: money that's actually gone (purchases minus refunds).
- **Refund**: money back for something you bought.
- **Transfer**: moving your own money around, like a TNG top-up. Not spending.
- **Debt**: money you owe a friend, or a friend owes you.
- **Write-off**: giving up on a debt. If a friend never pays you back, it counts as spending.
- **Bill**: a payment you know is coming, like laundry every 2 weeks. Tap "Mark paid" when you pay it.
- **Money in**: money you receive: from family, meal money, from a friend (a gift; money you must pay back is a loan, "I borrowed"), part-time pay, a gift. Not spending.
- **Wallet check**: you type how much money you really have (cash, TNG and bank together); Brokely compares it with your records since your last check.
- **Spent before Brokely**: one entry for what you spent this month before you started using the app.

## Your progress
- **Left in plan**: budget minus what you've spent.
- **Daily average**: how much you spend per day so far.
- **Logged day**: a day where you recorded something or tapped "No spending today".
- **Forecast**: a guess (shown as a range) of your total spending by the end of the cycle. Shown after two weeks of mostly logged days.
- **Pace**: whether you're on track or over pace.

## The app
- **Brokely mascot**: a broccoli that grows money instead of leaves. Its picture changes with how you're doing, and it grows as you earn XP. It doesn't talk.
- **XP**: points you earn for planning, logging and checking in.
- **Estimate**: an amount you fill in for days you forgot to log. Clearly marked; it never counts as a logged day.
- **Offline mode**: the app works without internet and without an account. It syncs later.
- **Pending import**: a TNG payment the app caught, waiting for you to confirm it.

## Words we don't use
allowance, balance, target, reserve, overallocated, Buddy.
