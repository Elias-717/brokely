# Brokely: features and what to advertise

**Who this is for:** the Brokely author and any AI agent writing ads, store text or social posts.
**Release covered:** 0.1 (Android, Malaysia, first pilot at AIU). App version 0.1.1. Last checked: 24 Oct 2026 (up to D-123; matches SRS v1.8).
**Keep it current:** when a feature ships or changes, update section 3 and, if needed, sections 4–6.

## 1. Rules for anyone writing about Brokely (read first)

These come from the product rules (AGENTS.md section 4, glossary, D-07, D-23). An ad that breaks one is wrong even if it sounds good.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Say **"plan"**, **"left in plan"**, **"to spend today"**, **"money in"**, **"from family"** | Say **"balance"**, **"allowance"**, **"target"**, "track your bank account" |
| Say Brokely helps you **plan** and **see** your spending | Promise "you'll never go broke", "save RMx", "guaranteed" |
| Say **no bank login, no PIN, no OTP** | Suggest it connects to banks, TNG or payments (it doesn't) |
| Say **works offline** and **no account needed** to start | Say "AI-powered" or "smart AI" (there is no AI in the app; smart categories are simple rules on the phone) |
| Be fun and kind; fact first, then the joke | Shame spending ("stop wasting money on bubble tea") |
| Use RM and Malaysian student life (mamak, dobi, Grab, TNG, hostel) | Use made-up reviews, user counts, ratings or "#1" |
| Show real screens from the current build | Show features from section 6 (not built yet) |
| Call the mascot **Brokely**, a broccoli that grows money instead of leaves | Call it a "buddy" (retired word) or say it talks |

**Google Play listing rules (metadata policy):** no "best", "#1", "top", "free" or prices in the **title, icon or developer name**; no emoji in the title; no claims of performance or ranking you can't show; no testimonials you can't verify. Limits: title **30** characters, short description **80**, full description **4,000**, **2–8** phone screenshots, feature graphic **1024 × 500**.

**Language:** the app is in English only (the privacy notice is also in Bahasa Melayu). Ads may use Malay or Manglish for reach, but screenshots show English.

**Price:** not decided in this file. Don't mention a price or "free" until the author confirms it.

## 2. Brokely in one breath

**One line:** A student budget planner that covers your essentials first and tells you what's left to spend today.

**Longer:** Brokely helps Malaysian university students plan the month's money, put meals, laundry and other essentials first, log spending in seconds (even offline), keep track of money owed to and from friends, and see honest numbers about how the month is going. Brokely, a broccoli that grows money instead of leaves, grows as you keep the habit.

**Who it's for:** students living on a monthly sum (from family, PTPTN, a scholarship, part-time work) who run out before the month ends, or don't know where it went.

**Tone:** friendly, a bit cheeky, never preachy. Vintage cartoon look: cream, ink outlines, broccoli green, mustard.

## 3. All features (in the app today, 0.1)

### Planning
| Feature | What the student gets | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Quick start chat** | Set up a plan in about a minute by chatting with Brokely: how much money, any debts from before, the dates. Mid-month? It asks what's left for the rest of the month, or plans the whole month (D-114). | First run |
| **Essentials first** | After quick start, Home suggests essentials one card at a time (Meals, Laundry, Home necessities, Transport, Phone & internet, Health), each with a suggested amount: Add, Later or Not for me. | Home |
| **Healthy plan tips** | Brokely shows what a healthy split of the budget usually looks like (e.g. about 45% food), as a tip, never forced. | Quick start, essential cards |
| **Set-asides** | Emergency and Savings money that's in the plan but not meant to be spent. | Your plan |
| **Guided setup** | Optional step-by-step plan with every category, "room for surprises" and a review. | More → Your plan |
| **Plans can change** | Edit the plan any time; the first version is kept, and the end-of-month summary shows original → final → actual. | Your plan, plan summary |
| **Next plan from your own history** | When a plan ends, amounts are suggested from what you really spent. | Next plan |
| **Your own categories** | Type a name under Other (e.g. Haircut) and it's offered next time. | Add |

## 4. What makes Brokely different

Compare with a typical expense tracker (a list of transactions and a pie chart). Phrase these as "Brokely does X", never "the only app that…".

1. **It plans, not just tracks.** You decide the month first; every number is about your plan, not a bank balance.
2. **Essentials come first.** Meals, laundry and home necessities are planned before treats, and the heads-up tells you when treats overtake meals.
3. **"To spend today" in one number.** Not a monthly total to divide in your head: what's fine to spend *today*, adjusted daily.
4. **Built for students' friend money.** Splits, IOUs, pay-back dates and write-offs are part of the plan, and paying back is never counted as spending twice.
5. **Honest numbers.** Forecasts only when there's enough data, shown as a range; no fake precision, no shaming.
6. **A mascot that rewards the habit, not starving yourself.** Brokely grows from logging, never from spending less.
7. **Made for Malaysia.** RM, mamak, dobi, Grab, TNG top-ups, meal money from family, a mid-month start.
8. **Private by design.** Works offline with no account; no bank login; export or delete any time.
9. **Fast to log.** Keypad with a calculator, notes that pick the category, drafts if it closes, Undo instead of pop-ups.

## 5. What to advertise (recommended)

### 5.1 Top features, ranked
Lead with the problem students feel ("money runs out before the month does"), then the answer.

| Rank | Feature | Why it leads | Proof on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Left to spend today** | Answers the student's real question in one number; nobody else's Home looks like this | Home budget card with the ring |
| 2 | **Essentials first** | The clearest reason to choose Brokely over a generic tracker | Essential card (Meals or Laundry) on Home |
| 3 | **Brokely grows with your habit** | Memorable, shareable, makes the app feel like a game | The 3 stages side by side, or Home with a proud Brokely |
| 4 | **Log in seconds** | Removes the "tracking is a chore" objection | Add sheet with the calculator keypad and a guessed category |
| 5 | **Split and IOUs** | Very student, very common pain ("who owes me for the Grab?") | Debts: Owed to me / I owe |
| 6 | **Honest insights** | Shows where the money went without guilt | Insights doughnut + heads-up card |
| 7 | **Bills & subscriptions** | Useful, but every app has bills; support, not lead | Bills with Mark paid |
| 8 | **Private, offline, no bank login** | Builds trust; best as the last screenshot or in text | A simple trust slide (no screen needed) |
